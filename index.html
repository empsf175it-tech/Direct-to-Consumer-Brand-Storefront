Florentine Craft & Kyoto Alchemy

The Chronicle of Sutura

Founded in Florence in 1988, Sutura was established on a radical proposition: that true haute couture should reject assembly lines in favor of individual artisan signatures.

1988

Via de' Tornabuoni Salon

Elena Sutura opens her private studio in central Florence, tailoring architectural overcoats for European patrons.

2014

The Kyoto Dye Union

Partnership with master dyer Kenji Takahashi to introduce 18-dip Sukumo fermented indigo and wild saffron extraction.

2026

Direct-to-Patron Era

Bypassing wholesale distributors. Every garment is allocated directly to discerning patrons in 64 nations.
